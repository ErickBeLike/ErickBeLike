# Hi there, I'm Erick! 👋

## Backend Developer with an API-First Philosophy

I'm a final-year **Software Development & Management Engineering** student at the Technological University of the Center of Veracruz (UTCV), with a deep-seated passion for backend architecture.

My approach to software is built on an **API-First methodology**. I thrive on architecting business logic, refining services, and designing endpoints that are not just functional, but a pleasure for frontend developers to consume. While I've forged myself as a full-stack developer, my heart lies in building the powerful, resilient, and scalable engines that drive great applications.

From the world of gaming, I bring a core belief to my work: **"Everything is tryhardable."** When I commit to a goal, I pursue it with relentless dedication, constantly iterating and improving until I achieve mastery.

---

### 🔭 What I'm Currently Building

* I'm currently architecting and developing **[ShopIn API](https://github.com/ErickBeLike/shopin-api)**, a feature-rich, API-first e-commerce platform designed to showcase a production-grade, secure, and scalable backend system.
